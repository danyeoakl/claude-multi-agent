## Codex and Grok
- Use Codex and Grok whenever they help, research above all (both are good at it). In ultracode the head session decides; a workflow step uses them only when its brief says so.
- Codex research: `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -C <scratch dir> -c web_search=live -o <out.md> - < <brief.md>`, in the background.
- Run a Codex review (the codex-review skill) mainly after your own work, at the end of a big chunk such as a branch before its PR, not after each change.
- When I ask who has built something, or to find other people's projects, code, people or posts, whether or not I mention X or Twitter (e.g. "who's built an MCP server for Airtable?"), use the x-research skill.
